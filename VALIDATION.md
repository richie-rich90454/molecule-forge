# Validation

## Geometry pipeline

Production geometry targets RDKit ETKDGv3 3D embedding from canonical SMILES, with stereochemistry (R/S, E/Z), formal charges, aromaticity perception, and dominant tautomers assigned before embedding. The in-app procedural embedder uses the same inputs (graph, bond orders, charges, stereo flags) with CSD-derived bond lengths and ideal hybridization geometries, followed by constrained relaxation. Every record carries provenance naming its source.

## Automatic checks

`MoleculeValidator` runs over all 389 records in `bun run validate` and in the test suite. It checks:

- every element is known and every bond index is in range, with no self bonds;
- valence limits hold (aromatic bonds count once, charged atoms get +1, hypervalent P and S get +2);
- no two atoms are closer than 0.5 angstroms;
- no covalent bond exceeds its limit (2.3 angstroms, 2.4 for metals);
- the atom inventory matches the molecular formula exactly;
- the computed mass matches the stored mass within 0.6 Da.

## Complex molecules

Drugs and cofactors with twenty or more heavy atoms are transcribed from PubChem connectivity SMILES through `SmilesParser`, and the parsed formula must match the published formula before the record ships. Fused-ring graphs (PAHs, steroids, porphyrins) are hand-verified for ring size, fusion edges, and hydrogen counts. Peptides, oligosaccharides, oligonucleotides, and polymers are generated by builders whose formulas are computed from the graph itself, so they match by construction.
